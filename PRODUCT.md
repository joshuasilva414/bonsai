# Product

## Register

product

## Users

College students studying data structures and algorithms from scattered course material. They use Bonsai to turn syllabi, notes, slides, and learning goals into a coherent path and to understand what to study next.

## Product Purpose

Bonsai is a graph-backed adaptive textbook. It builds a durable model of a subject and a private model of the learner, then uses both to create a living course outline and adapt future study sessions. Success means a learner can move from unstructured material to a useful, inspectable learning path that becomes more relevant as they study.

## Brand Personality

Thoughtful, calm, and intellectually rigorous. Bonsai should feel like a private professor: attentive and quietly confident, never theatrical or overeager.

## Anti-references

Bonsai should not resemble a generic AI dashboard, a chatbot with education branding, classroom-management software, a flashcard streak tracker, or a static course catalog. Avoid decorative automation theater and claims that make adaptation feel magical or uninspectable.

## Design Principles

- Keep the learner's material and goal at the center of every workflow.
- Make generated structure legible and inspectable rather than magical.
- Reduce cognitive load so the learner can focus on the subject.
- Use calm progress feedback while preserving a clear sense of system state.
- Let the product earn confidence through useful structure and precise language.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for core flows. Use semantic page structure, keyboard-operable controls, readable contrast, non-color status cues, polite live-region announcements, and reduced-motion alternatives. Prefer practical improvements in the normal implementation path over separate ornamental accessibility work.
