# Curriculum context

## Curriculum

A saved learning plan belonging to one learner. A curriculum has exactly one Subject as its root.

## Subject

The broad area a Curriculum teaches. A Subject contains Topics.

## Topic

A major division of a Subject. A Topic contains Subtopics.

## Subtopic

A focused division of a Topic. A Subtopic contains Learning Objectives.

## Learning Objective

An observable outcome the learner should be able to achieve. A Learning Objective is a leaf in the Curriculum tree.
